Those bumps on your key are a password, written in metal.

Inside the lock, every pin stack is split in two. The right key lifts every split to the same line, and only then can the plug turn. Linus Yale Jr. put it in writing in 1865: the tumblers "must all be arranged by the key, so that the various lines of division ... are all in the same line before the cylinder ... can be turned."
