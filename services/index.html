Questions

I'm not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You never have to log in to anything. If you want a change, like new timings, prices or a festival offer, send me a WhatsApp message and it's done.

Why should I pay before seeing anything?

You don't. I build your site first and send you a link. You pay the setup fee only when you're happy with it.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name. If you ever stop the monthly plan, I hand everything over to you.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Keeping the site running and secure, your updates, and keeping your Google listing accurate. There are no extra hosting bills.

Will the AI assistant say something wrong to my customers?

It only answers from the information you give it: your prices, menu, timings and policies. If a question isn't covered, or a customer is upset, it says it's passing the message on and alerts you. You can read every conversation, and I adjust it whenever you want.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes, any time, with one month's notice. No lock-in.

Will I show up on Google?

I set up your Google Business Profile and make the site easy for Google to read. Showing up for "near me" searches also depends on reviews and how active your listing is, and I'll help with both.
