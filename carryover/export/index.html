How to export your Microsoft To Do lists

Microsoft To Do retires on 30 June 2027. Here is how to get a copy of everything in it today, in files you own.

Short answer: Microsoft has promised its own export before To Do closes but has not said how it will work, or whether other apps will be able to read it. Carryover exports every list now: sign in with read-only access, pick a format, download.

Step by step

  1. Open Carryover

    Go to the Carryover tool on a computer or phone.

  2. Sign in with Microsoft

    Use the account you use for To Do. Microsoft will ask you to allow read-only access to your tasks. Carryover cannot change or delete anything.

  3. Choose lists and a format

    All lists are ticked. Untick any you don’t need, and choose “Include completed tasks” if you want your history too.

  4. Download

    The file goes to your downloads folder. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Questions

Should I wait for Microsoft’s own export?

You can do both. A backup now costs nothing and protects you if Microsoft’s export arrives late or in a format your next app can’t read.

Does it include completed tasks?

Only if you tick “Include completed tasks”. Most people leave them out when moving to a new app.

What about work or school accounts?

Carryover works with them if your organisation allows apps to read tasks. Microsoft is moving business users to Planner, so check what your IT team recommends.

Is Carryover safe?

It asks Microsoft only for permission to read your tasks. Your lists go from Microsoft straight into your browser and the file you download. There is no Carryover server, and the code is public on GitHub.
