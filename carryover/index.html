Moving to your new app

      
Download the matching format above, then follow the steps. Menu names can change as apps update.

      

        

          
Todoist
Format: Todoist
          
  1. Download. You get a zip with one CSV per list.
  2. In Todoist, create a project for each list.
  3. Open the project menu and choose Import from CSV, then pick that list’s file.

        

        

          
Notion
Format: Notion, Excel, Sheets
          
  1. Download the spreadsheet.
  2. In Notion, choose Import, then CSV.
  3. Your tasks become a table you can filter by list or due date.

        

        

          
Obsidian
Format: Markdown
          
  1. Download the Markdown file.
  2. Move it into your vault folder.
  3. Each list is a heading with checkboxes, steps nested underneath.

        

        

          
Excel or Google Sheets
Format: Notion, Excel, Sheets
          
  1. Download the spreadsheet.
  2. Open it directly, or use File, then Import in Sheets.

        

        

          
Thunderbird and Nextcloud
Format: Calendar tasks (.ics)
          
  1. Download the .ics file.
  2. Use the app’s import option for calendars or tasks.

Questions

      
Before you sign in

      
When does Microsoft To Do shut down?
Microsoft has said the To Do app will be retired on 30 June 2027 and has asked personal users to choose another app. It has promised its own export but has not said how it will work or whether other apps can read it. Sources: Windows Latest, Thurrott.

      
Can Carryover change or delete my tasks?
No. It asks Microsoft only for permission to read your tasks (“Tasks.Read”). Microsoft shows you exactly that before you agree, and you can remove the permission any time from your Microsoft account’s privacy settings.

      
Where do my tasks go?
From Microsoft directly into this browser tab, then into the file you download. Carryover has no server and keeps no copy. The sign-in token lives only in this tab and is gone when you close it.

      
Does it work with work or school accounts?
It can, if your organisation allows apps to read tasks. Some organisations block this; your IT team can tell you. Microsoft is moving business users to Planner, so check what your organisation recommends.

      
Is Carryover made by Microsoft?
No. Carryover is an independent, free tool. Microsoft and Microsoft To Do are trademarks of Microsoft Corporation.

      
I found a problem or my new app isn’t listed
Tell us which app you’re moving to and what went wrong, and we’ll add or fix it: open an issue on GitHub.
