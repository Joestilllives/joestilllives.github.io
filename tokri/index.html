Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no server and no database: your whole menu is packed inside the link itself, so it costs nothing to run. That's also why there's no sign-up.

How do I change my prices later?

Open your edit link (or come back to this page on the same phone), make changes, and share the new link. Old links keep showing the old menu, so update the link in your bio too.

Do customers need an app?

No. The link opens in any phone browser, and the order goes through the WhatsApp they already have.

Who can see my number?

Anyone you share the link with, the same as giving them your WhatsApp number. Use your business number if you have one.

Can I get online payments, photos or my own website?

Tokri is kept simple on purpose. For a full website with photos, UPI payments or a booking system, see what I build for businesses.
