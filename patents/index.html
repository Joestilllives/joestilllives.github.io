Second Chance Patents

Expired patents whose ideas were ahead of their time, matched with today's technology to make useful, affordable products for underprivileged communities. Expired patents are in the public domain, so anyone may build them.

How this works & how to add more patents (free)
  1. Find candidates. Open the BigQuery sandbox (free, no card), paste the query below, check the size estimate in the top-right is under 1 TB, and run it.
  2. Export. Click Save results → CSV and share the file.
  3. Analyze. Each patent is read and scored: what blocked it, what has changed, a product idea, who it helps, cost, and risks.

Score (0–100) = impact on people 35% · feasibility today 25% · low build cost 20% · market/demand 20%.
