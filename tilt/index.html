Tilt

Your phone is the instrument.

  • 📐
    Tilt forward and backChanges the note. Tip the top up to go higher.
  • ↔️
    Roll left and rightChanges the tone, from dark and soft to bright.
  • ✋
    Touch anywherePlays the note. Two fingers add a harmony, three play a chord.
  • 💥
    ShakeHits a drum.
