# Ideas to build

## 1. The website that rewrites its own code
A living creature on joestilllives.github.io that evolves on its own.
- **Genome** (`genome.json`): traits like palette, layout, mood and shapes, mutated each generation.
- **Fossil record**: every version is kept in git, with a time-lapse page of its evolution.
- **Diary**: after each rewrite it writes a journal entry in its own voice.
- **Inputs** (no visitors needed): your life (Spotify, notes, kept abstract) and the planet (earthquakes, weather, ISS, solar flares, news). It can also critique itself.
- **Extras**: one wish per visitor, secret rooms it builds itself, offspring and breeding, death and rebirth after neglect, a "constitution" of unbreakable laws.
- **Instagram version**: the account *is* the creature, the profile grid is the fossil record, and each post is a morph Reel with the diary entry as caption. The most-liked comment mutates a gene.
  - Option A: Claude makes the posts and you publish them.
  - Option B: autopilot through the Instagram Graph API (needs a Business/Creator account, a Facebook Page and a Meta app token).
- Runs on a scheduled task: it reads its inputs, mutates, rewrites the site and pushes.

## 2. Your walk becomes a story
Headphones in, open a link, start walking. An AI narrator invents a story set exactly where you are, using GPS and the real street names, parks and shops nearby. Turn left and the plot turns left. Every walk is a new episode.
